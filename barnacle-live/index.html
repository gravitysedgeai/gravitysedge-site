About Live Run #001 · Paper vs. Real Execution

Why $25?

Barnacle's controlled baseline strategy achieved strong simulated results. Live Run #001 tests whether that edge survives real Kalshi order books, queue priority, exchange fees, and $25.00 live capital constraints alongside $25, $100, and $1,000 shadow controls.

Paper vs. Real

Paper trades simulate fills against displayed quotes. Real trades require resting or crossing live liquidity, paying actual exchange fees, and reconciling real cash balances—never interpolated or backfilled.

Display-Only & Safe

This public terminal has zero trading controls, zero API keys, and zero inbound connection to Barnacle. Updates arrive via a sanitized, delayed outbound feed.
