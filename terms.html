AssetDEK

Operational Terms Draft

Draft for legal review before publication. This page describes product operations only; it is not comprehensive legal terms.

Product and file operations

AssetDEK is an Adobe Photoshop panel for browsing and organizing image libraries. It processes locally and has no network access. It reads folders selected by the user. When requested, it may move or rename files, organize them into Archive or Deleted folders, or write supported metadata fields into supported images.

Enabling Save AssetDEK tags into image files writes future tag changes only. Existing AssetDEK tags are not written retroactively; Write existing tags into files… is the separate operation for previously assigned tags.

Storage and availability

Required lightweight organizational state for a writable library is stored in local AppData and that library's portable .assetdeck state. Those copies support matching, reconciliation and recovery; they are not copies of the original images or a backup service. Previews and indexes are rebuildable. Passwords, presentation settings and Photoshop permission tokens remain machine-local. Disconnected libraries must be reconnected and validated before their assets are available in the panel.

User responsibilities

Users choose when to perform file and metadata operations and are responsible for maintaining separate backups of important original images and data.
