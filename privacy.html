AssetDEK

Privacy Policy

Last updated and effective: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AssetDEK, a plugin for Adobe Photoshop published by GravitysEdge Productions ("Gravity's Edge", "we"), handles information. In short: AssetDEK works on your computer, does not require an account, does not include analytics or tracking, and does not send your images or library information to us or anyone else.

What AssetDEK processes on your computer

To show and organize your libraries, AssetDEK reads, on your computer only:

  • the folders and drives you add as libraries, including file and folder names, locations, sizes and dates;
  • your images, to show previews and image details;
  • metadata embedded in your images, such as titles, keywords, camera details, location (GPS) data, AI-generation details and Content Credentials, when present;
  • the organizational information you create in AssetDEK, such as favorites, notes, tags, display names, locks and saved searches.

All of this processing happens locally during normal operation.

What AssetDEK stores, and where

AssetDEK stores its own information on your computer and in your libraries:

  • Application data (AppData) on your computer: settings, organizational records and a local recovery copy of them.
  • .assetdeck folders inside each writable library: organizational records, information used to recognize files that were moved or renamed, and optional previews and indexes. On Windows these folders are visible; on macOS they are normally hidden because their names start with a dot.
  • Lock password: stored only as a salted hash in Adobe's secure plugin storage on your computer.

An .assetdeck folder can reveal how a library is organized (for example notes, tags and file names). Review it before sharing a library or drive with others.

What AssetDEK sends

AssetDEK itself sends no information over the internet. It does not request network access, does not contact our servers, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

AssetDEK changes your image files only when you choose an operation that does so, such as moving, renaming or archiving files, or the optional, off-by-default metadata writing. Details are in the Terms of Service.

Support

We receive information only if you contact us. When you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it only to respond to you and to improve AssetDEK.

Settings › About › Export support report saves a small file on your computer with version numbers, settings, counts and recent errors. It is designed to leave out images, passwords, notes and file names. The file stays on your computer and under your control; it reaches us only if you choose to attach it to a message. You can open and review it before sending.

Adobe

AssetDEK runs inside Adobe Photoshop and is distributed through the Adobe Marketplace. Purchases, payments, licensing and downloads through the Adobe Marketplace are handled by Adobe; we do not receive or store your payment card details. Information you share with Adobe, and any information Adobe Photoshop or Creative Cloud collects, is governed by Adobe's own privacy policy.

Removing your information

You can remove AssetDEK's information at any time: delete a library's .assetdeck folders, remove libraries in Settings, or uninstall AssetDEK and delete its application data folder if it remains. Deleting this information does not delete your images, but it does remove your AssetDEK organization (such as favorites, notes and tags). To have us delete a support email, contact us.

Children

AssetDEK is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
