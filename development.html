AssetDEK · Development Log

Not just promises.
Actual progress.

Here's what we've improved, what we're working on, and what's really available through Adobe Marketplace. We separate development builds from public releases so you can see the work without mistaking upcoming changes for features you already own.

Last reviewed October 24, 2026 · Current Adobe Marketplace baseline: 3.2.0

October 24, 2026

AssetDEK 3.2.3 Development · Not submitted

Reliability and workflow improvements tested in Photoshop 2025 and 2026.

  • Scroll and focus: Mouse-wheel input continues to work when returning from other applications; choose 1×, 3×, or 5× wheel scrolling.
  • Smarter gallery sizing: Zooming preserves your position; tall images retain their correct proportions.
  • Duplicate finding: Choose folder, subfolder, library or all-library scope; clearer keep rules and visual identification of extras.
  • Preview storage: New .adpv preview files reduce clutter in other image browsers; stronger recovery after write failures, safer indexes and bounded fallback cache.
  • Maintenance and support: A consolidated Maintenance tab, Technical Support Package and diagnostic tools.
  • Interface: More readable filenames, improved card-size slider and refinements to folder and settings controls.

Real-input verification is recorded in the development changelog. Some visual details are still awaiting final approval; this build is not yet on Adobe Marketplace.

October 16, 2026

AssetDEK 3.2.2 Development · Not submitted

  • Batch Open and Batch Place for multiple selected images.
  • Read-only protection when another AssetDEK session is working with a shared library.
  • Gallery resize shortcuts with + and −, plus smallest/largest size controls.
  • Distinct UI Colors accessibility option.
  • Organizational-data fixes prepared in 3.2.1, including saved-search removal and synchronization reliability.

This version incorporates the fixes from 3.2.1 but was not separately submitted to Adobe.

October 15, 2026

AssetDEK 3.2.1 Superseded internally

  • Fixed saving favorites, tags, notes, labels and locks after removing a saved search.
  • Improved library organization synchronization and recovery.
  • Fixed a stuck missing-reference removal flow and overlapping metadata confirmation.
  • Added What's New information inside Settings.
October 2026

AssetDEK 3.2.0 Published on Marketplace

  • Indexed image metadata for browsing and search.
  • Generation and provenance details in the inspector, including supported C2PA information.
  • Opt-in tags and supported metadata editing, with guarded file writes.
  • Safer previews for large images.

This is the latest version confirmed by the repository as submitted and published at the time of this update.

Need help with an issue?

AssetDEK includes a support-report export. Development builds add a Technical Support Package with diagnostics. You choose whether to send the report, and the support report is designed not to include your images, passwords, notes or filenames.
