Built around your files, not around a cloud

AssetDEK works locally with the libraries you choose. Browsing, searching and reading metadata stay on your machine, and file-changing actions happen only when you ask for them.

Your organization travels with the libraryImportant organizational records are stored locally and alongside writable libraries, helping AssetDEK reconnect your work when drives move between systems.
External drives feel at homeReconnect a drive or library and AssetDEK validates it before bringing the assets back into your workspace.
Local by designNo account, analytics or tracking. Images and embedded metadata stay on your machine.
You decide when files changeBrowsing, organizing and reading metadata do not rewrite your images. Metadata writing and supported metadata editing are separate opt-in actions.

Questions? Good.

Here are the things worth knowing before you bring AssetDEK into your Photoshop workflow.

Is it a subscription?

No. AssetDEK is a one-time purchase with no subscription or yearly fee. Your purchase includes free updates throughout AssetDEK 3.

Which Photoshop versions does it work with?

Photoshop 2023 (version 24) and later. It is tested with Photoshop 2025 and 2026.

Windows or Mac?

Both. AssetDEK is a standard Photoshop (UXP) plugin for Windows and macOS, and every version is reviewed by Adobe before it appears on the Marketplace.

Does it change or move my images?

Only when you explicitly choose an operation that does so. Browsing, previews, search, favorites, notes and reading metadata do not change image files. Enabling Save AssetDEK tags into image files writes new tag changes to supported PNG and JPEG files from that point forward. Existing AssetDEK tags are not written retroactively; to write previously assigned tags into existing images, use Write existing tags into files…. Metadata editing is a separate opt-in control. Move, Rename, Archive, Delete and Restore happen only when selected.

Where are my favorites, notes and tags kept?

For each writable library, required organizational records are transactionally stored in local AppData and that library's portable .assetdeck state. The Portable setting controls optional previews, indexes and caches only. AppData is recovery storage, not a backup of original images; keep separate backups of important files.

What can I see when a library is disconnected?

The source remains listed so you can reconnect it, but its assets and folders do not appear in galleries, placeholders, cached previews, search or saved-search results. Saved searches keep their definitions and use only connected, validated sources. Reconnecting restores eligible results after validation and reconciliation.

Are previews originals?

No. Generated previews are rebuildable display representations. For a connected, validated library, AssetDEK checks portable preview storage first, then AppData, then regenerates from the original. Open, Place and file operations require the live original.

What metadata can it find?

Titles, descriptions, keywords, creator, copyright, locations, camera details, AI prompts, models, generators, seeds, steps, CFG, samplers, IPTC AI fields and C2PA Content Credentials when they are present in the file. Metadata can contain private information such as prompts or GPS locations; AssetDEK keeps it local.

Does it work with external drives?

Yes. Add or Locate the folder after reconnecting the drive; AssetDEK validates and reconciles its records before assets return. A read-only library remains browseable, but organizational changes requiring both stores are disabled. .assetdeck is visible on Windows and normally hidden on macOS due to its leading dot.

What if I move or rename files in Explorer or Finder?

AssetDEK usually finds them. When an image with favorites, notes, tags or locks moves or is renamed outside AssetDEK — even into another library — it checks only files of exactly the same size, proves the match by the file content, and reconnects everything, previews included. It never guesses between identical copies; anything it cannot prove waits under Missing for you to Relink.

Which file types does it show?

JPEG, PNG, GIF, PSD, PSB, TIFF, WebP, BMP and many camera RAW formats (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF and more). Animated GIFs play directly in the gallery. AVIF and HEIC are listed with placeholders.

Does it go online or collect data?

No. AssetDEK does not require an account, analytics or internet access. Embedded metadata is processed locally; the original is authoritative, and stale cached values are suppressed after source changes. See the privacy policy.

Is AssetDEK a backup system?

No. AppData and portable .assetdeck state support recovery of organizational data, not image files. Keep separate backups of important originals. See the Terms of Service.

What are “locks”?

A privacy convenience: locked folders and images show as placeholders inside AssetDEK until you enter your password. They don't encrypt or hide the real files from other programs.

How do I get help?

Settings › Help explains every feature; the same topics, with screenshots, are in the User Guide. For support, email gravitysedge@gmail.com and attach the file from Settings › About › Export support report (it contains no images, passwords, notes or file names).
