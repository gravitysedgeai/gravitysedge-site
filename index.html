How it works

Add the folders you already use. AssetDEK stays beside your Photoshop canvas and keeps the view up to date.

1Add folders and drivesChoose any folder on an internal or external drive. Its images and subfolders appear as a visual gallery—no importing or catalog.
2Browse and findUse Quick View, search, sort, groups and filters to narrow a large library without leaving Photoshop.
3Open or Place the originalDouble-click or choose Open, or use Place to add the original to the active document as an embedded Smart Object. AssetDEK never substitutes a preview.

Organize the files you already have

Work with real folders and files from the panel: create, move, rename, label and clean up. Click any picture to see it large, or “Learn more” for details.

Find anything fast

Search the information you already have, change how the gallery is arranged, and use embedded metadata as one more powerful way to find the right asset.

Portable, local and safe

AssetDEK never modifies your image files unless you explicitly enable metadata writing.

Portable .assetdeck recordsFavorites, notes, tags, display names and the search index can travel with each library in its hidden .assetdeck folder.
External drives and RelinkLibraries survive new drive letters and other computers. If something moves outside AssetDEK, Relink reconnects only a proven match.
Local onlyNo internet access, account or tracking. Images and embedded metadata—including prompts or GPS data—are processed locally.
Writing is opt-inBrowsing and metadata reading do not modify image files. PNG/JPEG tag and descriptive-field writing are separate controls, both off by default.

Questions

The things people usually ask before buying.

Is it a subscription?

No. AssetDEK is a one-time purchase — no subscription, no yearly fee. It includes free updates for AssetDEK 3.

Which Photoshop versions does it work with?

Photoshop 2023 (version 24) and later. It is tested with Photoshop 2025 and 2026.

Windows or Mac?

Both. AssetDEK is a standard Photoshop (UXP) plugin for Windows and macOS, and every version is reviewed by Adobe before it appears on the Marketplace.

Does it change or move my images?

Only when you ask it to. Browsing, previews, search, favorites, notes and reading metadata never touch image files. Saving tags as keywords and editing title, description, creator or copyright are separate options under Settings › Metadata, both off by default. Move, Rename, Archive, Delete and Restore do exactly what they say; Archive and Delete only move files into folders beside the originals.

What metadata can it find?

Titles, descriptions, keywords, creator, copyright, locations, camera details, AI prompts, models, generators, seeds, steps, CFG, samplers, IPTC AI fields and C2PA Content Credentials when they are present in the file. Metadata can contain private information such as prompts or GPS locations; AssetDEK keeps it local.

Where are my favorites, notes and tags kept?

In AssetDEK's own data file on your computer, with a copy in a small hidden .assetdeck folder inside each library so they travel with your folders. Settings › Backup saves everything to one file.

Does it work with external drives?

Yes. Each library remembers itself on its own drive. Plug the drive into another computer — or get a new drive letter — and AssetDEK recognizes it with all its records. A drive that is not connected simply shows “Not connected”.

What if I move or rename files in Explorer or Finder?

AssetDEK finds them. When an image with favorites, notes, tags or locks moves or is renamed outside AssetDEK — even into another library — it checks only files of exactly the same size, proves the match by the file content, and reconnects everything, previews included. It never guesses between identical copies; anything it cannot prove waits under Missing for you to Relink.

Which file types does it show?

JPEG, PNG, GIF, PSD and PSB, TIFF, WebP, BMP and camera RAW (CR2, CR3, NEF, ARW, DNG, RAF and many more). AVIF and HEIC are listed with a placeholder.

Does it go online or collect data?

Never. AssetDEK has no internet access, no account and no analytics. Indexed metadata stays in each library's hidden .assetdeck folder, which travels when you copy or share the library. See the privacy policy.

What are “locks”?

A privacy convenience: locked folders and images show as placeholders inside AssetDEK until you enter your password. They don't encrypt or hide the real files from other programs.

How do I get help?
