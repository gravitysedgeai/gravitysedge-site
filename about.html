About GravitysEdge

Built from experience.
Made for the way people work.

GravitysEdge brings more than 25 years of hands-on creative and technical experience to software that solves real workflow problems. We're creators building for creators.

Before the code, there was the work.

Our roots are in graphic design, video production, digital imaging, and teaching people to use creative technology. That includes professional Adobe training and expertise across Photoshop, Illustrator, After Effects, Premiere, InDesign, and earlier generations of creative tools.

Working with these applications for decades gives you a front-row seat to the small frustrations that add up. GravitysEdge grew from the conviction that those problems deserve practical, thoughtful solutions.

What we believe

Useful software shouldn't become another bill you have to pay forever.

Buy once. Use your version.

Our goal is a straightforward one-time purchase, not a mandatory subscription. AssetDEK 3 purchases include 3.x updates at no additional charge. New major versions may be optional paid upgrades.

A perpetual license doesn't override compatibility requirements or Adobe Marketplace terms. See our Terms for details.

Trust takes follow-through.

We document what changes, test fixes against real workflows, and make support diagnostics available so problems can be investigated rather than guessed at.
